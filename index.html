About Me

My name is Ali Armani. I live in Babolsar, Iran, where I work as a web developer. From an early age, I've been fascinated by technology, computers, and the internet. In high school, I taught myself C++ out of pure curiosity. Later, during university, I explored Python and HTML.

At first, I struggled with focus — jumping between multiple topics at once. Everything changed when I discovered web development. The moment I started working with HTML and CSS, I knew I had found my true passion. I decided to specialize deeply in front-end development, and that focused approach made all the difference. It taught me the importance of commitment and helped me build real expertise.
