About Me

I'm Ali Armani, an aspiring Front-End Developer based in Iran, open to remote collaboration with teams and clients worldwide. I build clean, responsive, and user-focused websites with attention to both functionality and visual detail.

Before transitioning into web development, I spent two years in retail, where I learned to perform under pressure, collaborate effectively, and solve problems with a positive mindset. Today, I apply those skills to software development.

I've built projects such as LuxuryDrive, a luxury car configurator that emphasizes premium design and user experience, while strengthening my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git, and Figma. I'm also completing a degree in English Language Teaching, which has strengthened my communication skills for working with international teams.

I'm currently open to freelance and part-time remote opportunities, and I'd be happy to connect.
